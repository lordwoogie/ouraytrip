Good to Know

  • Water safety: the pool and lake sit right off the patio. With a 3-year-old and two babies on the move, keep someone on water watch whenever the doors are open, and keep the hot tub covered.
  • Strollers vs. carriers: strollers work at Crystal Bridges, the Amazeum and Lake Bella Vista Park; bring carriers for Tanyard Creek.
  • High chairs: restaurants need notice for a group of 11 with three little ones, so call a day ahead.
  • Saturday crowds: the trail races fill Bella Vista trailheads in the morning, and the Razorbacks game fills bars and roads in the evening.
  • Layers: mid-November evenings can be chilly on the patio and the trails.
